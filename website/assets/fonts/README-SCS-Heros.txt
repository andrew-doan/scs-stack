SCS Heros

These fonts are TeX Gyre Heros 2.004 by Boguslaw Jackowski and Janusz M.
Nowacki, which is based on URW Nimbus Sans L. They have been changed for
this site:

- cut down to the Latin and the Latin Extended characters, each in a file
  of its own
- converted from OpenType to WOFF2
- renamed SCS Heros inside the files

They are released under the GUST Font License, in GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt.
The original fonts are at
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros
and on CTAN at https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre-heros.

The site uses them in place of Helvetica on devices that do not have it.
