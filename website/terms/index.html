  • the effects of alcohol where a quest permits it;
  • conflict with, or the actions of, members of the public, transport staff, security or wildlife;

You must replace any property lost or damaged through your careless, reckless or negligent behaviour, including acts done while intoxicated.

By participating, you agree to being filmed or photographed by the Clubs. The Clubs may use your likeness in photographs, video or audio recordings taken or received during or after the Event for any legitimate purpose without further approval. If you do not wish to appear in published material, tell an organiser or photographer at the time or notify OrgCom via Discord, and the Clubs will make reasonable efforts to exclude or remove you. c) GPS tracking. The Clubs will use GPS files only to create event graphics. d) Privacy. The Clubs collect your name, contact details, team, any medical information you disclose under clause 2(d), and your Event submissions. This information is used only to run the Event, contact you about it and produce recap and marketing content. Medical information is shared only with OrgCom members who need it for safety.

8) Emergencies. In an emergency, call 000 first. Then notify OrgCom via the Event Discord server or the emergency phone number given at the Briefings. Report any injury, incident or near-miss to OrgCom on the day, even if it seems minor.
